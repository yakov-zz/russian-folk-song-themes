# 1.0 · What Russians Sang: public dataset

Generated by `site/build.py` from the project's consensus labels. 17,162 records.

- `songs.jsonl` / `songs.csv`: one row per song: id, collection, stratum, language, text (`text_clean`, as printed, OCR),
  themes and primary theme, flags, genre, speaker gender, era of content, how the label was settled and the three coders' votes
  (JSONL only), place (settlement only where printed with the song; guberniya/oblast, macro-region, `place_basis`), community,
  recording year or interval, publication year, scan page, `reprint_of` (first record of its reprint cluster) and the full
  citation of the edition. Editors' notes and commentary are not included. `text` is null where `text_published` is false.
- Variants: many books print several variants of one song. `song_type` names the variant group a record belongs to within its
  book (the id of its first record; a record with no known variant is its own type) and `type_size` is how many records in this
  file share it. To count each song once, keep one row per `song_type`, or weight each row by 1 / (rows of its `song_type` among
  the rows you use). Groups come from an LLM reading of consecutive records checked by word overlap, plus the editors' own
  variant links: about 91% of grouped pairs are true variants, and about a third of variants are missed, so type counts are an
  upper bound. The site's figures count records; `reprint_of` marks exact reprints.
- `linked_versions`: ids of records in OTHER books judged to be the same song (main songs; an LLM said "same song" and the two
  texts share at least 30% of their rarer words). About 87% of links are right (two readers, 149 pairs). Links are pairwise:
  chaining them joins different songs, so do not merge them into groups without checking.
- `aggregates.json`: every figure shown on the site.
- `admin_centres.csv`: coordinates used for the map (administrative centres of guberniyas/oblasts).

Theme names and definitions: the codebook (docs/CODEBOOK.md in the code repository).
