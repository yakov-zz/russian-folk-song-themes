How the themes differ

Each row is a group of songs; each cell is the share of that group’s songs with that primary theme. Darker means a larger share. Russian-language songs only, except the collection view, which lists every edition.

Region, community and era are largely fixed by the book: the Don songs are one editor’s, the Terek songs another’s. A difference between rows is a difference between collections first, and only possibly between traditions. Rows that rest on one or two books say so.

The books behind the difference

Three books hold 87% of the Soviet editions’ wedding songs: Leningrad oblast wedding (1985), Vologda oblast songs (1981), Songs of the Volga region (1959). The first is entirely one district’s old village wedding; the other two are field recordings from the Vologda and Kuibyshev regions.
