Chubinsky, 1874: 2,608 songs in Ukrainian

Collected by the ethnographic and statistical expedition to the “Western-Russian Krai”. Of the 1,531 songs printed with a place, 92% come from guberniyas now in Ukraine (Podolia, Poltava, Chernihiv, Kyiv, Volhynia, Kharkiv), 35 from Voronezh and 31 from Grodno and Minsk; 1,077 songs have no place.

One song from the book

Теренъ, теренъ та не хміль. Прийшовъ милий, та не мій. Душу мою пориває: Казавъ прийду, та й не має. Ой я сплю — милий сниться, Душа жъ моя веселиться. Прокинуся — ажъ немає, Ажъ серденько замірає.

Show English translation

Blackthorn, blackthorn, and not hops. A sweetheart came, but not mine. My soul is torn: He said he would come, and he is not here. Oh, I sleep, and dream of my sweetheart, And my soul rejoices. I wake, and he is not there, And my little heart sinks.

Translation, line by line: Claude (AI), checked by the site author. The original above keeps the spelling of the printed edition.

Recorded in Полтавская. Chubinsky, vol. 5 (1874). Open this song

One song from the book

1. Нэ топыла, нэ варыла, На прыпичку жар, жар, А як пиду с цього сэла, Комусь будэ жаль, жаль. 2. Нэ топыла, нэ варыла. На прыпичку каша. Прощавайтэ, дивчаточкы, Тэпэр я нэ ваша.

Show English translation

1. I did not light the stove, I did not cook, On the stove ledge, embers, embers, But when I leave this village, Someone will be sorry, sorry. 2. I did not light the stove, I did not cook. On the stove ledge, porridge. Farewell, my girl friends, Now I am not yours.

Translation, line by line: Claude (AI), checked by the site author. The original above keeps the spelling of the printed edition.

One song from the book

— Чево ты, девачка, ні плачіш? Ты сваю косачку утратіш. — Хоть я і косачку утрачу, Хоць я косачку утрачу, Белага лебедя спаймаю. — Ой, лібедь-лібедь Ванечка, Белая лібёдка Надзічка.

Show English translation

— Why, girl, are you not crying? You will lose your braid. — Even if I lose my braid, Even if I lose my braid, I will catch a white swan. — Oh, swan, swan, Vanechka, And the white swan-girl is Nadzichka.

Translation, line by line: Claude (AI), checked by the site author. The original above keeps the spelling of the printed edition.
