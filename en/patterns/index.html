Patterns

Nine ways to look at 8,188 Russian folk songs: by place, by book, by what the songs contain, and by how the three models read them.

These describe what editors chose to print, not the tradition as a whole. Each group shows how many songs and books it rests on, and says so where one book holds most of it. Figures are shares of themed main songs from Russian-language editions, reprints merged, unless a chart says otherwise.

Themes that travel together

The readers

Where the models disagreed

Three models read every song. Songs of hardship and the foreign land split them most, then the comic songs, satire and tall tales: fewer than four in ten of these are labelled unanimously. Religious verse, historical and prison songs are the easiest to agree on.
