How these shares are computed

Each bar is the share of songs whose primary theme it is. Reprints of the same text are counted once. Songs with Soviet content, songs too fragmentary to classify and garbled texts are left out. Hover a bar for the range across books: a 95% collection-cluster bootstrap interval, which shows how much the share moves between books, not the uncertainty about the tradition as a whole.

Method

How we read each song

Свѣтитъ мѣсяцъ передъ горницею, Заря снотухаетъ передъ свѣтлицею. Ходитъ дѣвица изъ терема въ теремъ, Носитъ дѣвица перинушки; Ситцевы подушечки перетряхиваетъ, Съ милыми подружками разговариваетъ: Ахъ, что жъ это добра молодца нѣтъ, Ахъ, что жъ это голубчика нѣтъ? Не былъ, не бывалъ, въ карагодѣ загулялъ, Съ краснымъ дѣвушкамъ забавился? А вотъ и молодецъ идетъ И съ собой дѣвицу ведетъ!

Show English translation

The moon shines before the best room*, The dawn is fading before the bright chamber*. A maiden walks from room to room*, The maiden carries feather beds; She shakes out the little cotton pillows, She talks with her dear friends: “Ah, why is the fine young man not here, Ah, why is my darling not here? He has not come, has not been, he lingered at the round dance*, Has he been amusing himself with the pretty girls?” But here comes the young man And he leads a maiden with him!

* Words a translation cannot fully carry, with their meaning in Dal’s dictionary (1863–1866):

горница (best room)
the clean half of a peasant house, across the entryway from the everyday living room: no sleeping shelves, a stove with a chimney, an icon in the corner. Also any room or chamber.
светлица (bright chamber)
a light, clean room with proper windows, where no cooking is done; also a small room built up high, like an attic room.
терем (room to room)
a tall dwelling or its raised upper part, like a tower. In a noble house the women lived on the upper floor, in the terem; Dal quotes the saying “birds in cages, maidens in terems”.
карагод (round dance)
a dialect word of the Ryazan, Oryol and Voronezh regions for khorovod, a round dance with singing.

Translation, line by line: Claude (AI), checked by the site author. The original above keeps the spelling of the printed edition.
